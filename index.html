The founder behind the system

Built by Ross W. Edwards.

ClientCapture began with a simple observation: many excellent businesses deliver a five-star service through a fragmented digital experience. Customers are forced to search across websites, social pages, messages, menus and booking links before they can act.

Ross combines systems thinking, commercial awareness and hands-on digital development to turn that fragmented journey into one clear front door. The goal is not technology for its own sake. It is a better experience for the customer and a more organised flow for the business.
